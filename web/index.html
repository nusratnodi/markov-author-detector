Markov Author Detector

A Markov chain learns how four authors write. It can write new text in their style and guess who wrote a passage it has never seen.

Picks each next word at random, weighted by how often it followed the last two words in the author's book.

Author

Each author's model scores how likely it is to have produced these words in this order. The highest score wins.

0 words

Takes a random 50-word passage from the last 20% of a book. The models never saw this part during training.

Guesses the author of every unseen 50-word passage and counts how many are right. Random guessing would get 25%.

Order = how many previous words the model looks at. Higher orders write better text but detect worse, because most longer word sequences never appear in the training book.
